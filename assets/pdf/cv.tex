\section{Teaching}
Responsible for organizing and/or lecturing the following courses at TU Graz:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Recommender Systems} --- fundamentals and algorithms: content-based and collaborative filtering (matrix factorization), hybrid, knowledge-based, and group recommendation; plus LLMs in recommenders, explanations, evaluation, and biases.
  \item \textbf{Configuration Systems} --- state-of-the-art configuration knowledge representation, interacting with configurators, and intelligent testing/debugging of configuration knowledge bases.
  \item \textbf{Object-Oriented Analysis and Design} --- requirements engineering, class diagrams, state charts, object--relational mapping, design patterns, automated testing, and software design principles.
  \item \textbf{Introduction to Structured Programming} --- foundational programming concepts and techniques.
  \item \textbf{Software Development Process} --- software process models, high-quality requirements, prioritization, and effort estimation.
  \item \textbf{Explanations in Artificial Intelligence} --- theoretical foundations of explainability in logic-based and sub-symbolic AI systems.
\end{itemize}
